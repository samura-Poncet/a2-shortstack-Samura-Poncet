
## Find your zodiac sign!
https://a2-samuraponcet.onrender.com/

My project takes in a user's first name, last name, and date of birth to find their zodiac sign. After the user enters their info, they click the find my zodiac sign button, and their zodiac sign shows at the bottom along with the symbol for each sign. 
In my server.js, I've included the dates for each zodiac sign along with the symbols. 
CSS positioning: CCS grid to center elements on the page. 
To use the application, enter the required information(first name, last name, birthday) and click "Find my zodiac sign"
